(03) AboutGabriele Lucia, known as oakvx

I started because I wanted to understand how things work.

Let’s say I haven’t stopped yet.

Base
Sassuolo, Italy
Education
Diploma in Computer Science and Telecommunications
University
Computer Engineering · UNIMORE
Craft
Junior developer and graphic designer

(i) Where I start from

I started by taking software apart to see what was inside. I wanted to understand how programs worked and to try making something of my own.

Over time I became more and more interested in problem solving: understanding what isn’t working, finding a solution and seeing the result in the program I’m building. It’s still the part I enjoy most.

(ii) Three sides

One person, two crafts that talk to each other.

01

Person

Curious by nature. I start from a simple question about how something works and end up reading far more than I needed to. I like learning foreign languages: I speak five.

02

Developer

Websites and web applications, server-side logic and relational databases: from CRUD to industrial automation. I’m interested in web development, databases and automation.

03

Designer

Visual content for social media and interface design. I adjust colours, spacing and small movements until code and graphics look like they were made by the same person.

(iii) Path

Six chapters, in order.

I

2021 — 2026 · Sassuolo

School

I attended IIS A. Volta in Sassuolo, where I graduated in Computer Science and Telecommunications in 2026. For my diploma project I built Beautify, a web management system in PHP and MySQL.

A real management system, not the usual exercise.

II

Sep 2023 — Jun 2026 · freelance

Websites for local businesses

I worked as a freelance web developer and SEO consultant around Sassuolo and Scandiano. I built websites for garages, beauty salons and tool shops.

I took care of interfaces, publishing and search engine indexing. I also set up the businesses’ social media pages.

Clients don’t want to know which framework you use. They want the site to show up on Google.

III

alongside

Graphics

I also do graphic work: I create visual content for social media and design interfaces. It’s the visual side of my work, alongside software development.

Code and graphics: two sides of the same job.

IV

May — Jun 2025 · Seville

Erasmus+ in Seville

During my Erasmus+ I worked at Starbotica in Seville: IT and teaching activities in an international environment, using English and Spanish every day.

IT support covered Windows environments, Active Directory accounts, local networks and peripherals. I handled hardware and software troubleshooting and technical documentation.

I came back more independent, and with much more fluent Spanish.

V

Aug — Sep 2026 · Sassuolo

Format SRL

Software and automation apprentice: I supported PLC programming and the development of software for industrial systems.

I wrote T-SQL scripts on Microsoft SQL Server for data backup, management and archiving, and studied how PLC logic, application software and databases connect.

Code that moves real machines teaches you to take nothing for granted.

VI

today · Modena

Today

I study Computer Engineering at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, and meanwhile I keep building things of my own.

BeautifyPHP / MySQL SynkNode.js / PostgreSQL / llama.cpp Brat AnimatorJavaScript / Canvas / WebCodecs
Not everything went smoothly the first time. But it went.

(iv) How I work

Three promises, in small print.

01

High standards.

I deliver something when I’d use it myself. Without being fussy: I won’t send an email back over a comma.

02

Loyal.

If something isn’t working I tell you right away, not the day before the deadline.

03

Approachable.

You can ask me anything, even the question that seems silly. I’ve probably asked it myself.
