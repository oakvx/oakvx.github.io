I got into programming out of curiosity. I wanted to understand how software worked and try to build something of my own.

Over time I became more interested in problem solving: understanding what isn’t working, finding a solution and seeing the result in the program I’m building.

2021 — 2026

School

I attended IIS A. Volta in Sassuolo, where I earned my IT & Telecommunications diploma in 2026. For my final project I built Beautify, a web CRM with PHP and MySQL.

My diploma project ↗

2023 — 2026

Websites for local businesses

From September 2023 to June 2026 I worked as a freelance web developer and SEO consultant around Sassuolo and Scandiano. I built websites for workshops, beauty salons and tool shops.

I handled interfaces, deployment and search engine indexing, and helped set up business social accounts.

Graphic design

I also work in graphic design, creating visuals for social media and designing interfaces. It’s the visual side of my work, alongside software development.

MAY — JUN 2025

Erasmus in Seville

During Erasmus+ I worked at Starbotica in Seville. I took part in IT and educational activities in an international environment, using English and Spanish every day.

IT support covered Windows environments, Active Directory accounts, local networks and peripherals. I worked on hardware and software troubleshooting and technical documentation.

Above all, the experience made me more independent. Working in a new setting helped me adapt and improve my language skills.

AUG — SEP 2026

Format SRL

At Format SRL in Sassuolo I worked as a software and automation apprentice. I supported PLC programming and software development for industrial systems.

I wrote T-SQL scripts on Microsoft SQL Server for data backup, management and archiving. In this setting I studied how PLC logic, application software and databases connect.

Now

I study Computer Engineering at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia. I continue experimenting with my projects.

I’m interested in web development, databases and automation. In my personal projects I also work on browser tools, such as Brat Animator.
