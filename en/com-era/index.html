What changed

  • The light background turned black: #050505, not #000.
  • Nunito gave way to three typefaces: Fraunces for headings, Archivo for text, Cascadia Code for dates and numbers.
  • The blue is gone: a single accent colour is left, crimson.
  • The still Mii in the opening became a WebGL point sculpture that breaks and puts itself back together.
