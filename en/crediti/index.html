Who made what.

Licences, origin and details of the materials used on this site.

Typography

  • Fraunces, The Fraunces Project Authors (Undercase Type), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Headings and display text; the local variable files keep the weight (100–900), optical size (9–144), softness and “wonk” axes. Licence: Fraunces-OFL.txt. Source: github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces.
  • Archivo, The Archivo Project Authors (Omnibus-Type), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Body text; the variable file keeps the weight (100–900) and width (62–125) axes, used for the condensed and expanded compositions. Licence: Archivo-OFL.txt.
  • Cascadia Code, Microsoft, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Metadata, captions and terminal. The local subset keeps ASCII, Italian accents and punctuation, without changing shapes or metrics; the derivative’s internal name is Oakvx Portfolio Mono. Licence: CascadiaCode-OFL.txt.

Avatar and point sculptures

The Mii and its poses are drawings by the author, supplied for this portfolio in place of personal photographs, and served locally from the avatars/ folder.

On the home page and in “About”, the still poses (*-still.webp, the first frame of the original animations) are read in the browser and recomposed as WebGL point clouds: the files are not modified. The organic sculpture is generated by code, with deterministic noise.

Project materials

  • Brat Animator: a real 1440 × 1000 px screenshot of Brat Animator 4.0, captured on 18 October 2026 from oakvx.github.io/bratAnimator (work/brat-editor.webp). The demo text typed into the editor is “gabriele lucia”, “codice e grafica”, “demo portfolio”. The interface was neither rebuilt nor retouched.
  • Synk / Synkris: promotional material supplied by the author (archive synk_site.zip, entry synk_fixed/og-image.jpg), resized from 1867 × 949 to 1600 × 813 px and encoded as WebP (work/synk-cover.webp). It is not a screenshot of a production management system.
  • Beautify: the original logo from the archive maturita-main.zip (beautify-logo.svg), unmodified. No authentic screenshot of the application was available.
  • Spinotto Felice: a real screenshot of the demo website (work/autoofficina-cover.webp).

The *-small.webp variants are proportional resizes, with no content changes. Origin, dimensions and checksums are in work/sources.json. These materials belong to Gabriele Lucia / oakvx or were supplied for his portfolio: public availability does not grant a general licence to reuse them.

The site

Art direction, layout, transitions, cursor and point sculptures are original: HTML, CSS and JavaScript written by hand, with a small WebGL engine and no libraries. No third-party graphic assets beyond those listed here.

The black cat, the crow and the duck in the footer are hand-drawn in SVG for this site: line, pivots and springs live in the code.

The commit counter in After hours uses a grid and pixel numerals drawn in code; the data are the public GitHub totals of oakvx and oakvxish.

The pistol in the page transition is Service Pistol by Mateusz Sadek for Poly Haven, CC0 licence; geometry quantized and textures recompressed to WebP (nero/pistola/LEGGIMI.md).

The technology logos in About and Style come from Simple Icons (CC0 licence); the marks belong to their owners.

The shelves, lamp, hand truck and cardboard box in the 3D Warehouse come from Poly Haven, CC0 licence: Steel Frame Shelves 01 by James Ray Cock, Hanging Industrial Lamp by Kuutti Siitonen, Hand Truck by Mutanzom3D and Cardboard Box 01 by Rahul Chaudhary; geometry quantized and textures recompressed to WebP (scripts/prepara_modelli_magazzino.sh). Crates, emblems and the warehouse keeper are drawn in code.

The 3D game, reachable from the entrance menu, uses three.js (MIT licence, included in the game).
