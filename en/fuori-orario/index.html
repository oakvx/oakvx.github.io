About me

After hours I write code with no particular purpose, mostly because I like figuring out how things work. Sometimes I rebuild something from scratch even though it already worked, simply because I have since found a better way. My computer stays on a little longer than it should. That is usually how it goes.

Four things I keep open after I log off:

  • Free time — Personal projects. They usually start with a small idea and end up becoming real tools, with a few more features than they needed.
  • Details — Interfaces. I adjust colours, spacing and small movements until the code and graphics feel like they came from the same person.
  • Languages — Italian, English, Spanish and Greek. One native language and three I have practised at different times, for different reasons.
  • Curiosity — One question leads to another. I start with a simple question about how something works and end up reading far more than I needed to.

Status: one more small refinement · online. When an idea really grabs me, I see it through.

Projects

A few more projects: three ideas I started to learn something, developed far enough to deserve pages of their own.

  • Brat Animator (Canvas, JavaScript) — a browser editor that turns LRC lyrics into animated lyric videos.
  • Synk (Node.js) — starts with a chat, understands the reason for a message and connects appointments and business data to Google Calendar and a dashboard.
  • Beautify (PHP + MySQL, diploma project) — a multi-salon management system for customers, services, appointments and availability.
