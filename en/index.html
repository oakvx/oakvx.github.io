(02) Work

Four projects. I have others, but these hold up.

A creative editor, a WhatsApp assistant, a management system and a showcase site. Different problems, same stubbornness.

  1. 01 Brat Animator Creative editor · browser Turns synced lyrics into lyric videos, all in the browser. Born as a game, now at version 5. The game got out of hand.

  3. 03 Beautify Management system · diploma project A management system for several salons: clients, services and time slots that don't step on each other. The database and backend are mine.
