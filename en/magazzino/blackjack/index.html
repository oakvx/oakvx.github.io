How to play

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Picture cards are worth 10, aces 1 or 11.
  • Six French decks, reshuffled when a quarter is left. The dealer stands on 17, soft 17 included.
  • Blackjack (ace and a ten-value card) pays 3 to 2. A win pays 1 to 1, a tie gives your bet back.
  • You can double on your first two cards (one more card, double bet) and split a pair once. No insurance.
  • The chips are fake and stay in your browser. If you run out, you get a refill.
