M.2Briscola

Briscola. Sicilian cards, no mercy.

How to play ↓

How to play

  • Forty Sicilian cards, three in each hand. The last card of the deck, face up, sets the trump suit.
  • You don’t have to follow suit. The strongest card of the suit led takes the trick, unless a trump shows up.
  • Order: ace, three, king, knight, jack, 7, 6, 5, 4, 2. Whoever takes draws first and leads next.
  • Points: ace 11, three 10, king 4, knight 3, jack 2. There are 120 in total: more than 60 wins.
