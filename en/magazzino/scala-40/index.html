M.4Scala 40

Scala 40. Open with forty, close with style.

How to play

  • Two French decks with four jokers, thirteen cards each. Every turn you draw a card and discard one.
  • Melds: three or four of a kind (same value, different suits) and runs of one suit, at least three cards. The ace goes before the 2 or after the king, never across.
  • You open by laying down at least 40 points: ace 11, picture cards 10, the rest their value. One joker at most per meld.
  • You can take the discard only if you have opened or if it lets you open right away. Once open you can add cards to the groups on the table.
  • You close by discarding your last card. The others take penalties for what they hold (joker 25): whoever reaches 101 is out.
