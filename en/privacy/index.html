(N.1) Notes — privacy

What I know about you. Almost nothing.

No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Here is exactly what stays in your browser and who sees your visit.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, pixels or ads. Fonts are served by the site itself, not by outside services. That is why there is no banner to accept.

What stays in your browser

A few preferences, saved on your device and never sent to anyone:

  • lang: the language you picked.
  • motion: whether you stopped the animations.
  • oakvx-api-…: a copy, valid for one hour, of the public GitHub and weather data, so they are not fetched on every page.
  • gioco-… and the fake chip balance: language, audio and settings of the 3D game and of the Warehouse games.
  • oakvx-ingresso and nero-sparo, only while the tab is open: whether you have already seen the entrance menu and the page transition.

You can delete all of it from your browser settings: the site keeps working.

Who else sees the visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages: like any server, GitHub receives your IP address and the technical details of the request (GitHub’s privacy statement).

Some public data is requested straight from your browser: api.github.com for commits and statistics, api.open-meteo.com for the Sassuolo weather at the top. These services also see your IP address and browser, but not your location: the coordinates sent are Sassuolo’s.

If you write to me

Your address and message stay in my inbox and I use them only to reply. If you want them deleted, just ask.
