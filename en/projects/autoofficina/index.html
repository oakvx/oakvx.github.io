The projectInterface

From an automotive website to a demo

The project brings together services, approach, demonstration reviews, frequently asked questions and contact details on a single page with section navigation.

I created the original structure and styling. Alberto Pecchini handled demo cleanup, the light theme, fictional content and images. Spinotto Felice is a fictional business, with no real purchases or bookings.

Navigation and interactions

The layout adapts to desktop and mobile. JavaScript handles the menu, FAQ accordion, entry animations and light/dark switching, with the preference saved in the browser.
