Synk.

Synk handles incoming WhatsApp requests and appointments. Behind the chat are an intent picker, a local database, Google Calendar and a dashboard for the people running the business.

The project

The chat is the front door: people can write naturally instead of navigating menus. The intent picker reads the request and takes them into the right conversation.

How it works

The message

The core receives the conversation message, normalises text and checks for duplicates. Chat context makes it possible to resume an existing booking flow.

Three requests, three paths

People can ask in their own words; Synk works out what they mean and opens the right conversation.

During the chat, it gathers the details needed before passing the request to the management system.

Where the data goes

Appointments land in the local database and Google Calendar, so the conversation and the people managing the calendar share the same schedule.

The database also holds the services, team members and other information about the business.

The dashboard lets the team update those records and see analytics about how the business is doing.
