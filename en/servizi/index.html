06Services

(06) ServicesFour areas, one person

What I do. And what you get.

Websites, software, graphics and IT support. Often for the same client, sometimes in the same week.

i.

Websites

Showcase sites and landing pages that load fast and show up on Google. From 2023 to 2026 I built them for garages, beauty salons and tool shops.

  • Showcase sites and landing pages
  • SEO and search engine indexing
  • Publishing and domain setup
  • Business social pages
  • Desktop and mobile, light and dark themes

Example: Spinotto Felice

ii.

Software and backend

The part you don't see: management systems, databases, automations and integrations. Built to hold up when something outside fails.

  • Management systems and dashboards
  • Relational databases: PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server
  • Integrations: WhatsApp, Google Calendar, APIs
  • Automations and scheduled jobs
  • Local AI models, when they make sense

Examples: synk, Beautify, yag

iii.

Graphics and social

The visual side, kept close to the code: what you post, what you click, what you remember.

  • Social media content
  • Interface design
  • Logos
  • Animated text and video

Example: Brat Animator, and this site

iv.

IT support

Computers, networks and peripherals that stop working at the worst moment. I did it every day at Starbotica in Seville, in English and Spanish.

  • Windows: setup, updates, repairs
  • Local networks and Wi-Fi
  • Printers and peripherals
  • Accounts and email
  • Hardware and software troubleshooting, written up

Example: Erasmus+ at Starbotica, 2025
