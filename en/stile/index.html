08Style

(08) StyleHow this site is made

Four colours, three typefaces. And a pistol.

The rules of the site, written down so I keep to them.

(i) Colour

Black rules. Red speaks rarely.

  • Black#050505
  • Warm white#E8E6E1
  • Crimson#C81035
  • Graphite#292929

(ii) Type

A serif that talks, a sans that works, a mono that keeps count.

Aa Aa

Fraunces

Titles · optical size up to 144, softness 50

Aa Aa Aa

Archivo

Text · width axis 62–125, used as a graphic element

Aa 01

Cascadia Code

Metadata, numbers, the terminal

(iii) Form

A workbench, not a dashboard.

  • 01

    Grid

    1px lines every 120px (80 on phones), fixed under every page. Crosshairs mark the corners of the openings.

  • 02

    Corners, not boxes

    Panels have four marked corners instead of full borders. No glass, no decorative gradients.

  • 03

    Scale

    Sizes follow min(1vw, 1.9vh): on a wide, short monitor they follow the height, so nothing explodes.

(iv) Motion

Few moments, done properly.

  • 01

    Point sculptures

    A small WebGL engine with no libraries: an organic sculpture, a Mii in six poses, a 404. Press and hold and they break apart.

  • 02

    The bullet

    Between pages a 3D pistol fires: damped-spring recoil, slide, casing, and a bullet engraved with “oakvx.” that flies at you.

  • 03

    Off switch

    With reduced motion or “Pause animations”, all of it switches off. The site still works.
